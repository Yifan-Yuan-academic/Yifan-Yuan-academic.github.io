I am a first-year PhD student in Electrical Engineering at [King's College London (KCL)](https://www.kcl.ac.uk/), supervised by Yansha Deng and based in London, United Kingdom.

My research interests include video compression and artificial intelligence.

Before joining KCL, I received my Master's degree in Information and Communication Engineering from [Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT)](https://www.bupt.edu.cn/). I received my Bachelor's degree in Telecommunications Engineering with First Class Honours through the joint programme between BUPT and [Queen Mary University of London (QMUL)](https://www.qmul.ac.uk/) in 2023.

My technical interests include video coding, machine learning, PyTorch, Linux, and reproducible research workflows. Outside research, I enjoy table tennis, badminton, travelling, jogging, learning Japanese, working out, and cooking.
